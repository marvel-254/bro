# Privacy Policy for BRO

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-02
**Domain:** bro.omixsystems.store

BRO is a free, open-source social communication platform (GPL-3.0-or-later). This privacy policy describes what data is handled, by whom, and your rights.

## 1. Data We Collect
- Account info (username, email, profile) processed by Supabase Auth
- Messages and conversation metadata (participants, branches, timestamps)
- Presence and activity signals (online status, typing)
- Optional media uploads (images, files)

## 2. How We Use It
- To deliver real-time communication, presence, and discovery
- To power Pulse, People, Spaces, and Conversation Branches
- We do not sell data, use ads, or share with third parties beyond required infrastructure (Supabase)

## 3. Your Rights
- You may export or delete your account via the app
- You may request data deletion by contacting the maintainer
- All backend mutations verify authorization explicitly

## 4. Open Source
Source code is available on request while the project is in private development.
