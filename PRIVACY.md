# Privacy Policy for BRO

**Last Updated:** 2026-09-16

BRO is a free and open-source social communication platform. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

## 1. Data We Collect

### Account Information
When you create a BRO account, we collect:
- **Username** — your public display name
- **Email address** — for account identification and recovery
- **Display name** — your real or preferred name
- **Profile information** — bio, avatar, interests (optional)

This data is processed by **Clerk** (https://clerk.com), our authentication provider. Clerk stores and processes your account data on their servers in accordance with their Privacy Policy.

### Message and Conversation Data
- Messages you send and receive
- Conversation metadata (participants, timestamps, branch structure)
- Attachments (images, files) you upload

This data is stored on BRO's backend servers and is encrypted in transit.

### Device Information
- Device type and operating system version (for compatibility)
- Push notification tokens (for delivery of notifications)

We do **not** collect:
- Precise location data
- Contact lists
- Phone number (unless voluntarily provided)
- Usage analytics or tracking data
- Advertising identifiers

## 2. How We Use Your Data

- **Authentication**: To verify your identity and keep you signed in securely
- **Communication**: To deliver your messages, notifications, and updates
- **Account Management**: To provide and improve BRO services
- **Safety**: To detect and prevent spam, abuse, and harmful activity

## 3. Third-Party Services

BRO uses the following third-party services:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy |
|---------|---------|---------------|
| Clerk (https://clerk.com) | Authentication and user management | https://clerk.com/legal/privacy |
| Convex (https://convex.dev) | Database, real-time sync, and hosting | https://convex.dev/legal/privacy |

Your data is shared with these providers only as necessary for their services.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

- All data is encrypted in transit (TLS)
- Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage
- Passwords are never stored on BRO servers (handled by Clerk)
- We implement industry-standard security measures

## 5. Your Rights

As a user of this open-source software, you have the right to:
- **Access** your data
- **Export** your data at any time
- **Delete** your account and all associated data
- **Modify** or **correct** your profile information
- **Inspect** the source code and modify the application
